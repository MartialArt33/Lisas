Skills are saved, step-by-step workflows the AI follows the same way every time. They work in Claude and ChatGPT. The two entries below are samples only, taken from the original setup, to show what a skill does and how one is described. They are not installed for you. How skills work and how to build one

Find the right skill

  • Start with a Best Bet or pick the skill that matches the job you need help with.
  • Use the filter to narrow the list by recommendation or type of work.
  • Use the search box when you know a keyword, tool, or topic.

Use the detail pane

  • The detail pane explains what the selected skill does and how to use it.
  • Check what files, links, or details are needed before you start.
  • Use the kickoff prompt to begin, then review the output and follow-up steps.

Build your own

  • Ask the AI: "Build me a skill that..." and describe the job.
  • The AI drafts the skill and tells you how to install it in that app's settings.
  • Add an entry for it here with the Skills Page Maintainer sample as your guide.
