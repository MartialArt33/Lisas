Start a task mode fast

Modes are short bundles of directives you can paste at the start of a chat or any time the task changes to switch the AI into a focused way of working. Cheat Sheet cards change one behavior; a mode sets several at once. Use them for working style, not full workflows. Keep them lighter than Skills so they stay fast to scan, easy to apply, and easy to replace.
